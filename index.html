📸 How to Use the Photobooth

1

Start the Session

Option A: Smile at the camera (if smile detection is enabled)

Option B: Click the "START PHOTO SESSION" button

2

Get Ready

Position yourself in front of the camera. You'll see a countdown (3, 2, 1) before each photo.

3

Take 3 Photos

The photobooth will automatically capture 3 photos. Watch the countdown and smile!

4

Get Your Photos

After all photos are taken, you'll see:

  • QR code to download your video
  • Print button to print your photo strip
  • ⬇ Direct download link for your video
Tip: Make sure you're well-lit and centered in the camera frame for the best results!
